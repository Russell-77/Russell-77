## 🚀 Hackathons

### NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — MarsWay
*14–15 November 2026 · Sarawak Space Challenge · Challenge: Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map*

MarsWay is a planning and navigation web app for astronauts walking on Mars near Mount Sharp, Gale Crater. It plans the safest walking route, shows how far the crew can go and still get home, and tracks radiation.

- **My role:** Mobile Builder (phone Walk screen)
- **Built with:** React, Vite, Leaflet, PWA, Vercel
- **Team:** Elson, Kelly, Nigel, Mia, Aaron and me
- 🔗 [Live app](https://marsway-mu.vercel.app) · [Code](https://github.com/Russell-77/marsway)

### Dormathon 2026 — Morning Work Orders
*October 2026 · Team NR · Track 1: Predictive Model Track*

Morning Work Orders reads each machine's sensor readings and gives a maintenance technician a short ranked list of which machines to check first, what is likely wrong, why, and what to do.

- **My role:** AI Developer
- **Built with:** Python, scikit-learn, Streamlit
- 🔗 [Live app](https://track-1-predictive-ai-hackathon-b42hedqwgmeqnsng834wpc.streamlit.app) · [Code](https://github.com/Russell-77/Track-1-predictive-ai-hackathon)
